SEO Outreach & Link Building

Freelance | Present

Finding the right websites. Building the right connections. Getting quality links.

  • Find niche-relevant websites — Search for websites that closely match the client's industry, topic, and target audience.
  • Research different niches — Find suitable publishers across niches such as SaaS, technology, business, marketing, health, finance, lifestyle, education, and more.
  • Check website quality — Review niche relevance, traffic, content quality, website authority, and backlink potential before choosing a website.
  • Find the right publisher — Research website owners, editors, and relevant contacts who can approve guest posts or link placements.
  • Build guest post opportunities — Reach out to relevant publishers and secure guest post opportunities that fit the client's niche.
  • Find natural link placements — Look for existing articles where a client's link can be added naturally and make sense for the reader.
  • Handle outreach from start to finish — Manage emails, replies, follow-ups, content requirements, negotiations, and final placements.
  • Keep every placement relevant — Focus on niche-relevant websites and links that fit naturally instead of chasing random backlink numbers.

SEO Content Writer

Freelance | Present

Writing content that people want to read and search engines can understand.

  • Write for niches — Articles and listicles for topics like technology, business, marketing, lifestyle and other industries.
  • Research before writing — Study the topic, target audience and search intent before creating the content.
  • Create SEO content — Use keywords naturally while keeping the article easy and enjoyable to read.
  • Keep readers interested — Use headings, paragraphs, simple language and useful information to make content easy to follow.
  • Write for guest posts — Create high quality articles which is according to publisher guidelines and naturally support the clients backlink goals.
  • Make content useful — Add real information, examples, and clear explanations instead of filling articles with unnecessary words.
  • Match the websites style — Adjust the tone, structure and writing style to fit publishers and audiences.
  • Review before delivery — Check grammar, readability, keyword use, structure and overall quality of content before sharing .
